# MLA 9 Practice in This Profile

The `mla` profile serves the humanities. It extends the module core
and adds one package, `practice`, whose two rules carry the
format-family level of MLA 9: the author-page in-text shape, and the
works-cited list's alphabetical order with its completeness floor.

## Navigating the module

Style selection, when this family is the right choice, and the
benchmark procedure for doubtful cases, live in the core package's
`Style Selection Across the Disciplines` reference and the `Benchmark
Before Choosing` rule. This profile assumes the family is chosen and
carries its practice.

The deferred-pipeline posture for carried citation styles and locales
is taught once, in the core package's `Style and Locale Assets`
reference; no discipline profile carries style selection or asset
handling of its own.

## Metadata floor

Entry-level completeness is restated with the list shape in the `The
Works-Cited List` rule: all authors, year, title, venue, DOI or URL,
and access dates for volatile web content. When sources are stored as
ctxmod sources, the same floor maps onto the source metadata
vocabulary: author, issued, title, container or publisher, doi or
url, accessed.

## Edition

MLA 9 (the MLA Handbook's 9th edition, 2021) is the current edition,
with interim guidance published on the MLA Style Center; its August
2025 revision of the generative-AI template is summarized with the
other styles' treatments in the core package's `Citing AI-Generated
Content` reference.
