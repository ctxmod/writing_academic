# Limitations Discussion

Academic work requires explicit limitation acknowledgment. Limitations sections establish intellectual honesty and define the boundary conditions within which findings hold.

## Limitation Categories

Research limitations fall into distinct categories. Identifying the category structures the discussion and suggests appropriate hedging.

| Category | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Fidelity** | How well the study represents the phenomenon it models | Simplified model vs. real-world complexity |
| **Generalizability** | Whether findings extend beyond the study context | Single-site study, specific population |
| **Robustness** | Stability of findings under varying conditions | Sensitivity to parameter choices, edge cases |
| **Reproducibility** | Whether others can replicate the work | Data availability, methodological detail |
| **Resource constraints** | Computational, data, or time limitations | Sample size, training budget, study duration |

## Discussion Approach

Present limitations as boundary conditions rather than apologies. Each limitation should specify what it constrains, how it affects interpretation, and what would address it.

**Defensive** (avoid):
> "Unfortunately, our study has several limitations that prevent us from drawing strong conclusions."

**Constructive** (target):
> "Our analysis focuses on a single site; multi-site validation would establish whether the patterns generalize across institutions."

The constructive approach maintains credibility while precisely defining the scope within which findings apply.

## Section-Specific Application

Limitations appear differently across document sections:

**Methods**: Acknowledge methodological constraints when describing design choices. Justify why the chosen approach is appropriate despite limitations.

**Results**: Note where results may be affected by known constraints. Include negative findings—omitting them undermines credibility.

**Discussion**: Provide a dedicated limitations subsection addressing categories systematically. Connect each limitation to its impact on interpretation.

**Conclusion**: Summarize scope boundaries without restating every limitation.

## Proportionality

Limitation discussion should be proportional to the claims made:

- Strong, novel claims require thorough limitation coverage
- Incremental findings require proportionally less
- Speculative interpretations warrant explicit caveats

Match assertion strength to evidence strength throughout. Under-acknowledging limitations damages credibility; over-acknowledging undermines confidence in genuine contributions.
