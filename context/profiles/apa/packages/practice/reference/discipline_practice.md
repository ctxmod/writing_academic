# APA 7 Practice in This Profile

The `apa` profile serves psychology and the social sciences. It
extends the module core and adds one package, `practice`, whose two
rules carry the format-family level of APA 7: the author-date in-text
shape, and the reference list's alphabetical order with its
completeness floor.

## Navigating the module

Style selection, when this family is the right choice, and the
benchmark procedure for doubtful cases, live in the core package's
`Style Selection Across the Disciplines` reference and the `Benchmark
Before Choosing` rule. This profile assumes the family is chosen and
carries its practice.

The deferred-pipeline posture for carried citation styles and locales
is taught once, in the core package's `Style and Locale Assets`
reference; no discipline profile carries style selection or asset
handling of its own.

## Metadata floor

Entry-level completeness is restated with the list shape in the `The
APA Reference List` rule: all authors, year, title, venue, DOI or
URL, and a retrieval date for content designed to change. When sources are
stored as ctxmod sources, the same floor maps onto the source
metadata vocabulary: author, issued, title, container or publisher,
doi or url, accessed.

## Edition

APA 7 (the Publication Manual's 7th edition, 2019) is the current
edition. APA updates its guidance between editions on the APA Style
site; the September 2025 formats for generative-AI references are the
largest recent change, and they sit with the other styles' treatments
in the core package's `Citing AI-Generated Content` reference. The
"et al." rule (three or more authors, first author from the first
citation), the 20-author list limit, and the DOI-as-link form are the
APA 7 rules a draft carried over from APA 6 most often misses.
