# The APA Reference List

The reference list closes the document and holds every cited source,
ordered alphabetically by the first author's family name. The ordering
is the style's convention for typeset documents; a reader resolving an
in-text citation such as `(Strand, 2019)` searches the list under S
and expects one matching entry per cited work.

## The completeness floor

Every entry carries the fields the style manual requires for its type,
and the floor below applies before any entry is submitted:

- all authors, in the style's name order, up to the manual's display
  limit: APA 7 lists up to 20 authors, and for 21 or more gives the
  first 19, an ellipsis, and the final author;
- the year of publication;
- the title of the work;
- the venue: the journal, the book publisher, or the equivalent
  container;
- the DOI when one exists, written as a link (`https://doi.org/...`),
  otherwise the URL;
- a retrieval date only for content designed to change over time.

Incomplete metadata is the failure class that survives review most
easily: a missing DOI or an unnamed later author still reads as a
finished entry. Consistency is checked against the style manual
itself, not against habit. Punctuation, capitalization, and italics
follow the manual's current edition, applied identically to every
entry.
