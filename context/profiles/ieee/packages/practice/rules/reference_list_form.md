# The Numbered Reference List

The reference list closes the document and holds every cited source
under its number, in the style's own ordering: entries are numbered in
the order their works are first cited in the text. The ordering is the
style's convention for typeset documents; a reader resolving a
bracketed citation such as `[3]` expects entry 3 to be the third
first-cited work.

## The completeness floor

Every entry carries the fields the style manual requires for its
type, and the floor below applies before any entry is submitted:

- all authors, in the style's name order, up to the manual's display
  limit: up to six names, and for more than six the first author
  followed by "et al.";
- the title of the work;
- the venue: the journal, the conference proceedings, or the
  equivalent container;
- the year of publication;
- the DOI or URL when one exists;
- an access date for volatile web content.

Incomplete metadata is the failure class that survives review most
easily: a missing conference name or an unnumbered entry still reads
as finished. Consistency is checked against the style manual itself,
applied identically to every entry, abbreviation for abbreviation.

ctxmod machinery note: the tool's own rendered citations list is a
name-sorted mapping independent of any style's numbering convention.
The convention above binds the typeset document.
