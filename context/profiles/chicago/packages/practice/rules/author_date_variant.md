# Chicago 17: The Author-Date Variant

Chicago 17's author-date system cites as the APA family does at the
format-family level. The in-text citation is an author-year pair,
`(Strand, 2019)` in the parenthetical placement or `Strand (2019)` in
the narrative one, and a page or section locator for quotations rides
inside the parentheses after the year. The closing list is a reference
list ordered alphabetically by family name, one entry per cited work.

## When a venue prefers it

The two systems serve different venue conventions, and the venue
settles the choice, never the document's preference. A venue's author
instructions name the system when one is required; doubtful cases fall
to the benchmark, and the Chicago vocabulary helps: venues that say
notes or bibliography mean the notes-bibliography system, while venues
that say reference list or name-date mean author-date. The
verification procedure is this profile's navigation reference's
subject, and the benchmark itself is the core package's `Benchmark
Before Choosing` rule.
